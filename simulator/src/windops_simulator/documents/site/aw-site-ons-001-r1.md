## 1. Scope

How technicians and cranes reach the turbines at Atlas Ridge, a mountain ridge site, and how work changes in
winter. It applies with the manufacturer's safety procedure, WS-SAF-001; where the two differ, the stricter governs.

## 2. Access road

- The access road climbs 400 m from the gate. Four-wheel drive vehicles only, between November and March.
- After snow, nobody goes up until the road has been cleared and gritted, and the site manager has agreed.
- Sign in at the gate, and out again. The gate log is the site's record of who is on the ridge.

## 3. Crane work

| Condition | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Mean wind speed at the crane's jib height, for a main component lift | 10 m/s |
| Gusts, for any lift | 15 m/s |
| Ground | The hardstanding at the turbine, checked after heavy rain |

A main bearing or gearbox lift needs the large crane, which takes two days to bring up and rig.

## 4. Winter working

- Ice falls from blades and the nacelle as it thaws. Keep 150 m from a turbine that may carry ice, unless it is
  stopped with its blades checked clear.
- Do not climb a tower whose ladder or platforms are iced.
- In freezing weather, check the gearbox oil heater has brought the oil to 20 °C before the turbine restarts
  (WS-OM-4514, section 5).
