## 1. About this manual

This manual describes the WS-8.0-167, an 8 MW offshore turbine with a three-stage gearbox, and the maintenance it
needs. It is for qualified technicians and the engineers who plan their work. Procedures for specific faults are in
their own documents, listed in section 7; where they differ from this manual, the procedure's current revision
governs.

## 3. Operating states

The controller reports one operating state at a time:

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `producing` | Connected and generating |
| `idle_low_wind` | Below cut-in: the rotor idles, blades pitched to wait for wind |
| `stopped_high_wind` | Above cut-out: blades feathered, rotor stopped, until the wind falls back |

Below rated wind speed the controller holds the best tip-speed ratio, 8, and the blades stay flat. Above it, the
blades pitch out of the wind to hold rated power. The turbine also reports the status its operator sets, such as
`maintenance` or `offline`. In either, the rotor is held still.

## 4. Normal operating ranges

These are typical healthy values in service, ten-minute means. They depend on load and on the air around the turbine:
a hot day at full load sits at the top of each range, and is not a fault.

| Reading | Normal range | Alarm |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gearbox oil temperature | 45 to 60 °C | `GBX_OIL_TEMP_HIGH` at 63 °C |
| Gearbox bearing temperature | 50 to 68 °C | `GBX_BEARING_TEMP_HIGH` at 70 °C |
| Main bearing temperature | 25 to 40 °C | `MAIN_BEARING_TEMP_HIGH` at 42 °C |
| Generator bearing temperature | 40 to 62 °C | `GEN_BEARING_TEMP_HIGH` at 65 °C |
| Nacelle vibration, RMS velocity | 0.2 to 2.0 mm/s | `NACELLE_VIBRATION_HIGH` at 3.0 mm/s |

The controller alarm reference, WS-REF-ALM, gives every alarm's raise and clear levels.

## 5. Condition monitoring

Three accelerometers record the drivetrain: on the main bearing, on the gearbox at the high-speed stage, and on the
generator's drive-end bearing. Each records a 1.6-second window every 10 minutes. The CMS vibration interpretation
guide, WS-ENG-CMS-001, explains what each sensor hears and how faults show in it.

## 6. Maintenance schedule

| Interval | Task |
| --- | --- |
| 6 months | Visual inspection of the nacelle, tower and blades from the nacelle; check for oil and grease leaks |
| 12 months | Gearbox oil sample to the laboratory; replace the gearbox oil filter elements; clean the oil cooler's air side; check the cooler fan's operation and current |
| 12 months | Main bearing regreasing, and a sample of the purged grease for wear metals (WS-PRC-BRG-021) |
| 12 months | Verify the temperature sensors against a reference (WS-PRC-SNS-040) |
| 24 months | Blade inspection from the ground or by drone; pitch bearing and yaw system inspection |
| 60 months | Gearbox oil change, or sooner if a sample's results require it |

Oil and grease are lubricants of the grades on the nacelle's lubrication plate. Never mix grades.

## 8. Safety

All work at height and inside the hub follows WS-SAF-001. Isolate and lock the turbine before any work on rotating
or energised equipment. Lock the rotor before entering the hub. The site's own access procedure applies as well, and
where the two differ the stricter one governs.
