## Summary

Grease samples from WS-4.5-145 main bearings at turbulent onshore sites show wear metals rising faster than expected
in the second half of the regreasing interval. The interval is halved, from 12 months to 6.

## Affected turbines

All WS-4.5-145 turbines. The WS-8.0-167's interval is unchanged.

## Action

1. Regrease each main bearing every 6 months, under WS-PRC-BRG-021, with the quantity on the lubrication plate.
2. Take a sample of the purged grease at each regreasing.
3. Where a sample shows iron above 150 ppm, assess the bearing for wear under WS-PRC-BRG-021, section 4.

This bulletin overrides the 12-month interval in the WS-4.5-145 manual, WS-OM-4514, revision 2, until the manual's
next revision.
