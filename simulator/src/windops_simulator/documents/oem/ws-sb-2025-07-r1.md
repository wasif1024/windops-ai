## Summary

The run capacitor of the gearbox oil cooler fan motor has failed early on some WS-8.0-167 turbines, after about two
years in service. A failing capacitor lets the fan run slowly, so the cooler falls behind at high load and the oil
runs hot, raising `GBX_OIL_TEMP_HIGH` and then `GBX_OIL_TEMP_CRITICAL`.

## Affected turbines

WS-8.0-167 turbines commissioned in 2024. The WS-4.5-145 uses a different fan motor and is not affected.

## How it shows

- The gearbox oil runs hotter than its history at the same load, more so the higher the load.
- The fan's run signal is on, but its current is low, or its speed is below its rating.
- The gearbox bearing temperature rises with the oil.

## Action

1. At the next visit to each affected turbine, measure the capacitor's capacitance, and replace it if it is more
   than 10% below its rating, swollen or leaking. WS-PRC-GBX-012 will include the check at its next revision.
2. Where `GBX_OIL_TEMP_HIGH` has been raised in the last 90 days, check the capacitor first.

The replacement part is the uprated capacitor in the spare parts list. It needs no other change.

## Reporting

Report each failed capacitor found, with the turbine's serial number and its hours in service.
