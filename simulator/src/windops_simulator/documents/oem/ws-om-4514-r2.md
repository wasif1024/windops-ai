## 1. About this manual

This manual describes the WS-4.5-145, a 4.5 MW onshore turbine with a three-stage gearbox, and the maintenance it
needs. It shares its drivetrain design, sensors and controller with the WS-8.0-167, so most procedures apply to both.
Where they differ from this manual, the procedure's current revision governs.

## 3. Operating states and ranges

The operating states, normal ranges and alarms are those of the WS-8.0-167 manual, WS-OM-8167, sections 3 and 4.
Onshore sites see a wider swing of air temperature, by day and by season, than offshore ones, so temperatures move
more over a day. Judge a reading against the same hour on earlier days, not against a fixed figure.

## 5. Winter operation

At air temperatures below −10 °C, the gearbox oil heater holds the oil at 20 °C or more before the turbine starts.
Ice on the blades unbalances the rotor and shows as vibration at once per revolution; the controller stops the
turbine when its ice detection trips. Do not mistake ice imbalance for a mass imbalance from the blades themselves:
it comes and goes with the weather. See WS-PRC-ROT-030.
