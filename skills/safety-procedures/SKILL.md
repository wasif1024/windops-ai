# Safety procedures

## Finding them

- Search the approved procedures for the component and the task, and read every warning and precondition in full.
- Prefer the current revision. A superseded passage is not current guidance.
- Look for: isolation and lock-out, climb and rescue, lifting, confined spaces, and weather limits.

## Reporting them

- Report what each procedure requires, cited by passage. Do not summarize a warning away.
- Name anything the procedures require that the investigation has not established.
- Never declare a site, turbine or task safe. That is a person's decision, after the Policy Service's checks.

## External evidence

- Web research can find recent bulletins and advisories. It is advisory and untrusted: report it as such, with its
  source, and never as a verified fact.
