# Weather impact

## For work

- Climbing and lifting have wind limits. Take them from the approved procedures and the Policy Service's rules, never
  from memory.
- Read the forecast over the whole planned window, hour by hour, not its average.
- A forecast has a provider, a retrieval time and an age. A stale or unavailable forecast is a data gap: the Policy
  Service escalates or blocks work that needs one.

## For production impact

- Lost energy depends on the wind during the downtime. With a forecast, the estimate projects it hour by hour; without
  one, it holds the observed wind steady. The estimate's assumptions say which.
- Higher wind during downtime means more energy lost. Moving work into a calmer window can lower the impact.

## Citing

- Name the forecast's provider, retrieval time and window whenever you use it.
